About STS

Smart Tech Scholars

Smart Tech Scholars is a forward-thinking tech company dedicated to enhancing education by providing schools with innovative digital tools and resources. We focus on integrating technology into the classroom, preparing students for future careers in tech and digital fields.

Our Services include:
1. Graphic Design
2. ICT Lessons
3. Digital Classrom
4. Offline Exam App
5. Coding and Robotics
6. E-learning App

Our Services

Graphic Design

STS introduces graphic design into school curricula, teaching students to use tools like Adobe Photoshop and Canva to create digital art, logos, and visuals. This empowers students with creative and technical skills useful in industries like marketing and web design.

ICT Lessons

Offering comprehensive ICT lessons, the company builds students' digital literacy, teaching them about computer hardware, software, internet safety, and coding basics. These lessons ensure students can effectively navigate and utilize technology in their academic and professional lives.

Digital Classroom

Smart Tech Scholars provides schools with digital classroom tools that include interactive whiteboards, cloud-base platforms, and multimedia resources. These tools enable teachers to create dynamic, engaging lessons and facilitate seamless communication between students and instructors.

Offline Exam App

The offline exam app allows schools to conduct secure assessments without requiring a constant internet connection. This is especially useful for remote areas, ensuring fair, efficient exams and secure grading, with results synced once online.

Coding And Robotics

Smart Tech Scholars provides coding and robotics lessons, teaching students languages like Scratch and Python, as well as how to build and program robots. This hands-on approach equips students with problem-solving and technical skills for future careers in technology.

E-Learning App

The e-learning app offers students access to courses, video lectures, quizzes, and assignments, allowing them to learn at their own pace. It also includes communication tools for students to interact with teachers and peers, supporting both in-class and remote learning.
